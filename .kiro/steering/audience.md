# Audience and voice

- Reader: a stressed parent on a phone, maybe in line or on a bus.
- About a 6th-grade reading level. Short sentences. One idea per sentence.
- Warm, calm, never blaming. "Here's what you can do" not "You failed to".
- Every screen ends with one clear next step and a phone number.
- Big tap targets (min 44px). Works at 360px wide. No hover-only UI.
